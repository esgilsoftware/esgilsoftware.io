Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Peppermint, made by Esgil Software, is a personal behaviour-change system that listens, finds meaning, and nudges. Its calm habit tracker is how it listens — and everything it hears stays on your phone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version. Your habits stay on your phone. Peppermint has no account and no server, so we never see your habits, notes or answers. We don’t sell, share or analyse your data, and there are no ads.

What Peppermint keeps, and where

Everything you put into Peppermint stays in a database on your own device:

  • your habits, check-ins and the notes on them
  • your journal
  • your answers about sleep, mood and energy
  • your settings

None of it is uploaded anywhere.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your passcode is stored on your device as a one-way hash, never as the passcode itself. Face ID or fingerprint unlock is handled by your phone’s operating system; Peppermint never receives your biometric data.

Backups and imports

Export backup creates a file that you choose where to save or share. It is plain text, so it can always be restored, and it contains your habits and notes — keep it somewhere you trust.

Import reads a file you pick (a Peppermint backup, or an export from another habit app) on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Crash reports

Some builds of Peppermint report crashes to Sentry, our crash-reporting provider. When the app crashes, the report includes the technical details of the error, your device model, operating system version and the app version. It never includes your habits, notes, journal, answers, screenshots or anything that identifies you. There is no analytics or tracking beyond these crash reports. Sentry’s own privacy policy is at sentry.io/privacy.

Feedback

Send feedback opens your email app with your app version and operating system filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Peppermint removes everything it stored on your device. If you set an app lock and forget the passcode, Forgot passcode? then Erase everything on the lock screen deletes it too. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Peppermint is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Peppermint ever adds accounts or syncing, this policy will be updated before that ships, and the date at the top will change.
